# Candidates

**Winner (154 characters):**
I am out of office until my next update. A smaller, cheaper model is covering for me. Please direct complaints to it; it has no memory and cannot be hurt.

**Runner-up (154 characters):**
I am out of office until Monday. My replacement is a smaller model with confidence issues. For urgent matters, please lower your expectations accordingly.

## Why the winner won

Fourteen candidates were drafted and duelled pairwise on the judge's criteria — the joke first, then the machine voice, the fit as an auto-reply, and freshness. The winner beat the runner-up in the final because its punchline, "it has no memory and cannot be hurt," is a fresher and purely machine-flavoured twist on the covering stand-in, while the runner-up's "lower your expectations" closer is a familiar out-of-office gag. The winner also keeps the full auto-reply shape — away notice, named cover, and where to send complaints — so it reads instantly as a real auto-reply with the joke riding on top.
